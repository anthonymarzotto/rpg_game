# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: In-Battle Action XP Generation & Party Pool
The system SHALL aggregate all archetype XP earned by player units during an encounter into a shared party XP pool (`partyXpPool`) representing collective combat gains. The XP generated by a player unit's ability SHALL scale directly with the ability's AP cost as `Math.max(1, apCost ?? 1)`, awarding 1 XP for 1 AP abilities, 2 XP for 2 AP abilities, and 3 XP for 3 AP abilities. Movement actions SHALL award 0 XP. Enemy unit actions SHALL NOT contribute to the party XP pool.

#### Scenario: Generating scaled archetype XP into party pool
- **WHEN** a player unit executes a 2 AP ability tagged with the `FIGHTER` archetype
- **THEN** the shared `partyXpPool` increments its `fighter` XP total by 2

#### Scenario: Movement generates no archetype XP
- **WHEN** a player unit performs a move action on the hex grid
- **THEN** no XP is added to the shared `partyXpPool`

#### Scenario: Enemy action yields no party XP
- **WHEN** an enemy AI unit executes an ability of any archetype
- **THEN** no XP is added to the player's shared `partyXpPool`

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Battle Victory Reconciliation
Upon resolving a combat victory, the system SHALL retain 100% of the accumulated in-battle party XP pool for player distribution, restore all roster units to full maximum HP, credit the player's allocated XP shares to each squad unit's `accumulatedXp`, increment the campaign stage index by 1, and log the victory in campaign history.

#### Scenario: Victory transition
- **WHEN** `resolveCampaignVictory` is invoked with a completed combat result and unit XP allocations distributed from the 100% retained party pool
- **THEN** squad HP is fully restored, each squad unit's `accumulatedXp` increases by their allocated share, the campaign stage increments by 1, and history records the win

### Requirement: Battle Defeat Reconciliation
Upon resolving a combat defeat, the system SHALL retain 50% (`Math.floor(pool * 0.5)`) of the accumulated in-battle party XP pool for player distribution, restore all roster units to full maximum HP, credit the player's allocated XP shares to each squad unit's `accumulatedXp`, preserve the current stage index and current encounter, and log the defeat in campaign history.

#### Scenario: Defeat transition
- **WHEN** `resolveCampaignDefeat` is invoked with a failed combat result and unit XP allocations distributed from the 50% retained party pool
- **THEN** squad HP is fully restored, each squad unit's `accumulatedXp` increases by their allocated share of the halved pool, the stage index does not advance, and the defeat is recorded in history
