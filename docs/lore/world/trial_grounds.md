# The Celestial Trial Grounds

> *"The bells ring once to signal the opening salute; they ring twice when a bout concludes. Between those two chimes, you see the very best of mortal grit and grace."*
> — Master Herald Alistair

---

## Overview

Scattered across the skyways beyond [The Nexus](the_nexus.md) lie the **Celestial Trial Grounds**—a series of ancient floating amphitheaters, terraced plazas, and weathered stone arenas dedicated to the tournament bouts of the [Grand Conjunction](../cosmology/grand_conjunction.md).

Built from pale marble, river-slate, and carved granite, these arenas were designed for fair, honorable contest. Protected by gentle celestial wards that prevent fatal injury, the trial grounds allow traveling companies to push their martial and magical prowess to the absolute limit without fear of tragic loss.

---

## Anatomy of an Arena

### 1. Elevated Tiers & Terraces
The trial grounds feature varied terrain elevations—sun-bleached stone steps, low parapets, and raised dais rings.
* **Tactical Vantage:** Archers and mages seek higher terraces for clearer lines of sight and broader views of the field.
* **Grounded Steps:** Combatants who step down or leap from high ledges land with a resounding thud, cushioned by the buoyant starlight currents that wrap each platform.

### 2. Weathered Colonnades & Broken Archways
Centuries of wind and gentle star-rain have smoothed the columns and ruined arches lining the arena perimeters.
* **Cover & Maneuver:** Skirmishers slip between crumbling pillars to break line of sight, while shieldbearers use sturdy column bases to anchor defensive lines.

### 3. Spectator Galleries & Sideline Benches
Around the perimeter of every trial ground run raised stone galleries and low benches.
* **The Sideline Spirit:** Wayfarers not participating in the active bout, along with members of rival companies, gather along the balustrades to cheer good maneuvers, wave their banners, and offer fresh water to resting contenders.

---

## The Spirit of the Bout

Tournament matches in the Firmament are governed by centuries of honorable tradition:
* **The Opening Bow:** Before weapons are drawn or spells are cast, opposing squads offer a customary salute—a raised blade, a touch of the hat, or a bright pulse from their guiding Sprite.
* **Yielding with Honor:** When a combatant takes a solid blow that drains their stamina, they do not fight to the death. Instead, they gracefully yield or withdraw from the ring to sit beside the field, nursing bruises and watching their comrades continue the contest.
* **Post-Match Fellowship:** As soon as the final salute is given, competitors immediately shake hands, help fallen rivals to their feet, and trade respectful compliments on clever plays.
