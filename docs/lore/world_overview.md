# Astral Tactics — World Overview: The Starlit Conjunction

Welcome to the canonical world overview for **Astral Tactics**. This document outlines the foundational setting, the nature of the Wayfarers, the purpose of their journeys, and the spirit of the celestial trials.

---

## 1. The Realm & The Conjunction

* **The Firmament:** High above the mortal world lies the Firmament—an expansive celestial realm of perpetual twilight skies, floating starlit landmasses, weathered stone plazas, and quiet skyports.
* **The Grand Conjunction:** Once in a great era, the night skies align in an event known as the Grand Conjunction. Beams of gentle starlight cascade down toward the world below. Ordinary mortals who gaze upward at just the right twilight hour find themselves lifted along these radiant paths into the Firmament.
* **The Astral Tournaments:** The gathering is neither a war nor a crisis. The realm hosts ancient celestial tournaments—centuries-old trials where traveling bands test their skill, teamwork, and resolve.
* **The Falling Star’s Favor:** The ultimate reward awaiting at the pinnacle of the trials is the Falling Star’s Favor: an astral blessing potent enough to grant a single genuine, heartfelt wish to whichever company earns it (such as revitalizing an ailing home province, restoring an ancient landmark, or ensuring calm seas for a generation of voyagers).

---

## 2. The Wayfarers & The Novice

* **Everyday Wanderers:** The Wayfarers are not destined champions or hardened mercenaries. They are everyday folk—village squires, curious apprentices, herd-tenders, town guards, and restless wanderers—who were curious enough to follow the starlight upward.
* **Arriving as a Novice:** When a new Novice joins a traveling band, they have literally just stepped onto the starlight docks:
  * They arrive in whatever they wore back home—simple linen tunics, sturdy travel boots, and modest starter gear like a practice shield or a plain iron blade.
  * Wide-eyed and taking in the floating plateaus, they look around for a friendly camp to take them in.
* **Growth on the Trail:** Out on the trial grounds, traveling and standing beside their companions, they steadily discover their true capabilities. A Novice naturally grows into their own martial discipline or arcane study simply through experience, practice, and the bonds forged on the road.

---

## 3. The Opposing Bands & Tournament Spirit

* **Rival Companies:** Contenders are organized into a colorful variety of independent traveling companies and guilds from across the mortal realm—academy students testing their fieldwork, city watch patrols seeking honor, or guild artisans chasing a wish. Just like your company, each band has its own earnest reasons for entering the trials.
* **The Nature of Matches:** Engagements are structured tournament bouts. Defeated units simply yield or withdraw from the field to rest, tend to minor bruises, and watch from the sidelines. There is no grim slaughter or spiritual oblivion—only honest competition, sportsmanship, and shared celebration around evening campfires.

---

## 4. The Cosmic Sprite (Player Persona)

* **A Native Companion:** You are a **Cosmic Sprite**—a small, luminous celestial creature native to the Firmament.
* **Guiding the Company:** Because you are born of the stars, you have an intuitive sense for the celestial tides and flow of the field. You choose to travel alongside this particular group of mortals as their companion, scout, and guide.
* **Camp Life:** Around the fire, the Wayfarers treat you as a cherished friend and lucky presence, relying on your gentle guidance without ever treating you as an imposing authority.
