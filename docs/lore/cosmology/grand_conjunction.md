# The Grand Conjunction & The Falling Star's Favor

> *"Our grandmother told stories of the starlight beams that touched down on the miller's green fifty winters ago. When they descended again this autumn, I packed my pack, took my father's old billhook, and stepped into the light."*
> — Mara, Former Orchard Tender, now Novice Wayfarer

---

## Overview

Once in a great era, the wanderer stars, planetary spheres, and distant nebulae align in perfect harmony—an epochal celestial event known as the **Grand Conjunction**. 

As the alignment reaches its zenith, the barrier between the mortal earth and the heavens softens. Beams of luminous, warm starlight descend from the sky like shimmering ribbons, touching mountain meadows, quiet town squares, and country roads below. Ordinary mortals who gaze upward at the twilight hour and step into these radiant paths find themselves carried weightlessly upward, arriving at the starlight docks of [The Firmament](the_firmament.md).

---

## The Astral Tournaments

The Grand Conjunction inaugurates the ancient **Astral Tournaments**—a grand, peaceful tradition stretching back further than recorded history.

Across the floating amphitheaters and weathered stone arenas of the Firmament, traveling companies assemble to test their mettle, coordination, and spirit. These bouts are celebrations of mortal determination, where skill and mutual respect take precedence.

---

## The Falling Star's Favor

At the pinnacle of the tournament trials awaits the most coveted treasure in the heavens: the **Falling Star’s Favor**.

* **The Heartfelt Wish:** The Favor is a concentrated blessing of celestial grace, capable of granting a single profound, heartfelt wish to whichever company earns it through the trials.
* **Earnest Pursuits:** The wishes sought by traveling bands are as diverse and earnest as the mortals who make them:
  * Revitalizing an arid, ailing home province with fresh spring water.
  * Rebuilding a historic bell tower or ancient landmark shattered by past storms.
  * Ensuring calm seas and safe voyages for a generation of seafaring fishers.
  * Curing a rare family ailment or unlocking an archive of forgotten herbal lore.

---

## The Cycle of Return

When the Conjunction draws toward its eventual close, companies return down the starlight ramps to their homes across the mortal realm—richer in spirit, bound by lifelong friendships, and bearing the star-blessed gifts or wisdom won through honest contest.
