# The Firmament

> *"You feel it the moment your boots leave the starlight ramp: the air smells of cool night dew and sun-warmed stone. Look out past the docks and the horizon never truly ends—it just melts into gentle violet twilight."*
> — Thomas, Apprentice Blacksmith of Oakhaven

---

## Overview

High above the mortal world lies **The Firmament**—an expansive celestial expanse where floating landmasses drift across an endless sea of twilight. Suspended in tranquil balance, islands of ancient, moss-dusted granite, terrace gardens, and weathered stone plazas float peacefully beneath a dome of soft constellations.

The Firmament is not a battleground of warring gods or a realm of planar decay; it is an ancient, serene crossroad. For centuries, its quiet skyports and open plazas have welcomed travelers from across the mortal sphere whenever the stars align.

---

## Atmosphere & Landscape

### 1. The Perpetual Twilight
The sky of the Firmament is bathed in an enduring, tranquil twilight. There is neither the scorching heat of midday nor the pitch blackness of deep night. Instead, deep indigos, dusty violets, and rose-gold nebulae paint the horizon, casting a soft, clear radiance that makes lantern light warm rather than urgent.

### 2. Floating Landmasses & Gentle Tides
The landmasses of the Firmament—ranging from small rocky garden islets to vast plateaus carrying entire skyport districts—are buoyed by gentle stellar tides. Streams of pale blue starlight flow beneath the islands like quiet rivers, carrying mineral-rich stardust that nourishes hardy alpine mosses, pale night-blooming bellflowers, and silver-leaved rowan trees.

### 3. Weathered Stone Plazas & Ancient Skyports
Dotted across the larger plateaus are sweeping plazas, stone aqueducts, and wide open-air amphitheaters paved with smooth river-slate. Nobody knows which ancient artisans first laid these stones, but they were built to withstand eternity. Today, travelers gather at their edges to gaze down at the distant clouds or watch neighboring islands drift slowly past on the starlit wind.

---

## Life in the High Realm

* **Gravity & Sensation:** Movement across the floating islands feels grounded and natural, though a pleasant lightness accompanies each stride. Falling over the edge of a plateau does not cast a traveler into oblivion; the upward rush of starlight currents gently buoys wanderers back onto nearby perimeter ledges, leaving them with little more than a bruised ego and windblown hair.
* **Campfire Nights:** In the evenings, traveling companies pitch canvas tents along the edges of the stone plazas. Smoke from cedar fires rises into the violet sky as pots of warm broth simmer, accompanied by the quiet strumming of lutes and friendly banter between rival squads.

## The Sprite's Insight

To the native **Cosmic Sprites**, the Firmament is home. Where mortals see empty air between islands, a Sprite sees the invisible currents of celestial warmth—bridges of starlight, rising thermals, and gentle eddies that guide ships and foot-travelers safely between trial grounds.
