# The Astral Realm

> *"Look up into the deep slate of the firmament: those stars do not merely burn; they sing. Attune your ear, and you will find your blade strikes true."*
> — Master Astrologer Valen, *Treatise on Stellar Resonance*

---

## Overview

The **Astral Realm** is the vast, multidimensional medium that encompasses the world of *Astral Tactics*. Neither strictly physical nor purely ethereal, the astral realm is composed of crystallized stellar currents, drifting planar debris, and dimensional folds. 

Within this realm, natural physical laws yield to celestial resonance. Distance is non-Euclidean, geometry manifests in hexagonal energy matrices, and time advances in calibrated cadence rather than a continuous stream.

---

## Cosmological Principles

### 1. The Hexagonal Grid Matrix
The fabric of walkable astral space naturally stabilizes into interlocking hexagonal lattices. Scholars believe this hexagonal crystallization is caused by the interference patterns of three primary celestial resonance waves: **Force**, **Finesse**, and **Focus**. Where these waves intersect, stable astral ground forms; where they waver, open voids and jagged precipices yawn into the abyss.

### 2. Dimensional Tides & Planar Decay
The astral realm is in a constant state of flux. Calm pockets—such as [The Nexus](../world/the_nexus.md)—remain tethered to steady stellar beacons. However, venturing outward into deeper **Sectors** plunges an expedition into turbulent planar tides where reality itself begins to fray, spawning hazardous high ground cliffs, volatile elevation ridges, and void fissures.

### 3. Starlight & Eclipse
Starlight in the astral field is tangible matter. When gathered during triumphs, it coalesces into luminous crystals capable of unlocking dormant memories within wayfarers. Conversely, an **Eclipse** occurs when void entropy overwhelms a sector, severing wayfarers from their conduits and casting their spiritual remnants back to The Nexus.

---

## Tactical & Mechanical Echoes

- **Hexagonal Arena**: The geometric stabilization of the realm is the direct cause of the hex-based tactical battlefield (`src/core/hex/`).
- **Initiative & Cadence**: Time does not flow smoothly; instead, units tick through dynamic Initiative (CTB timeline) based on their attunement to Finesse currents.
- **Elevation Tiers**: Fluctuations in astral density create discrete terrain elevations (Tiers 0 through 3), granting vantage bonuses to those who command the heights and punishing unwary combatants with lethal fall impact damage.
