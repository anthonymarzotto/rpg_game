# The Celestial Trial Guardians

> *"Do not fear the stone sentinels. They were crafted by kind hands in an ancient age, built not to harm us, but to teach our feet where to stand and our shields how to brace."*
> — Constellation Keeper Orin

---

## Overview

Alongside matches against [Rival Companies](../factions/rival_companies.md), traveling bands in the Firmament must prove their coordination against the **Celestial Trial Guardians**.

These star-forged sentinels, brass clockwork mechanisms, and animated river-stone constructs were placed across the [Trial Grounds](../world/trial_grounds.md) ages ago by the architects of the Firmament. Functioning as timeless, impartial sparring partners, they test a company's readiness, defensive poise, and tactical harmony before the band advances to higher tournament tiers.
