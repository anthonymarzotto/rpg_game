# Rival Companies & Tournament Guilds

> *"They're not enemies; they're just folks from the other side of the mountains who love a good match as much as we do. When the bells sound, we give them our very best—and when it's over, the first round of cider is on whoever lost."*
> — Captain Rowan, Riverbend Watch Guard

---

## Overview

The trial grounds of [The Firmament](../cosmology/the_firmament.md) bring together a vibrant tapestry of **Rival Companies**—independent bands, guild delegations, and study circles that journeyed upward during the [Grand Conjunction](../cosmology/grand_conjunction.md).

Every company brings its own colors, earnest traditions, and heartfelt aspirations for the [Falling Star's Favor](../cosmology/grand_conjunction.md).

---

## The Culture of Friendly Contention

Between rounds at [The Nexus](../world/the_nexus.md), the boundaries between rival companies melt away:
* Contenders stroll through rival campsites to trade badges, embroidered pennants, and home-region recipes.
* Veteran competitors happily share sparring tips with younger squads who struggled in the morning matches.
* When a team secures a victory in a hard-fought match, their opponents are frequently the first to lead the round of applause.
